What is RapL Genie?

RapL Genie is a practical learning assistance tool that helps employees and students find relevant training materials quickly. Instead of digging through multiple modules or disconnected documents, learners can look up key topics directly and navigate straight to the lessons they need.

Designed for internal training environments, RapL Genie focuses on clear comprehension, concise lessons, and steady skill progression during everyday work.

Key Features

01

Personalised Learning

Directs learners to relevant courses and modules based on their current progress and assigned learning paths.

02

Knowledge Access

Provides straightforward access to corporate guidelines, tutorials, and technical resources from a single search point.

03

Simple Interaction

A practical, uncluttered interface that allows users to quickly find answers without unnecessary navigation steps.

04

Learning Support

Offers clear topic summaries and direct links back into active coursework to support steady completion.
